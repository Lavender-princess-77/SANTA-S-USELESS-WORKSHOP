# 🎅 Santa's Useless Workshop

> *Where productivity goes to hibernate*

Welcome to **Santa's Useless Workshop**, the ultimate cozy chaos of Christmas-themed nonsense! This is a place where logic takes a vacation, mini-games reign supreme, and absolutely nothing is serious… except the holiday vibes.

## ✨ What You’ll Find Inside

A delightfully absurd collection of **16 interactive mini-games and useless features**, each designed to entertain, confuse, and spark laughter:

* **🔘 The Button That Judges You** – Click it and face sassy judgment.
* **💬 Echo Chamber Mode** – An AI that agrees with everything you say (even the weird stuff).
* **🚪 Quit the Game** – Can you really escape… or is it trapped in a loop of chaos?
* **⚡ Vibe Check: The Game** – Test your festive vibes with absurd questions.
* **🧠 Overthinker Simulator** – Watch simple choices spiral into existential dread.
* **🪙 Decision Coin Flip** – A coin that refuses to cooperate and questions your life choices.
* **🛤️ Free Will Test** – Discover if free will exists… spoiler: maybe not.
* **🎄 Holiday Affirmation Generator** – Cozy words to warm your soul.
* **🎁 Holiday Gift Roulette** – Shake gifts, guess what’s inside (probably socks).
* **🎬 Unskippable Cutscene** – Patience, or frustration… your choice.
* **📚 Game That’s a Tutorial** – Learn nothing forever.
* **🔋 404: Motivation Not Found** – A quest for lost motivation.
* **📝 Procrastination Pro Max** – Track your epic avoidance skills.
* **💬 Game That Ends Mid-Sentence** – Literally… mid-
* **🧩 Dream Logic Puzzle** – Puzzles without logic, dream-style.
* **⬆️ Useless Upgrade Simulator** – Upgrade things that didn’t need upgrading.
